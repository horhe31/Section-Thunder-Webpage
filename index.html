Standard Operational FAQs

According to Section 23.1, the mandatory retirement age for employees is 60 years old. Extensions are only possible with express company approval.

Teaching staff must tender their resignation at least 22 calendar days before the effective date. Non-teaching staff must provide a 30-day notice to ensure a smooth transition of duties.

SIL is a yearly benefit of 5 days with pay after rendering at least one year of service. Unused SIL can be converted to cash.

Major offenses (like swearing at a student, misusing company property, or personal relationships with students) have no prescription period. Outright dismissal is often meted out for the first offense.

ATI follows an "All or nothing" scheme for perfect attendance. Maintaining perfect attendance adds a cumulative base of PHP 300 per cut-off, up to 6 months. The Annual ATI reward is PHP 3000.

Start your assessment almost at the end of class time. Ensure you verbally preview the assessment with the student before ending the class on time. Any additional notes must be placed at the topmost part of the Teacher's Note.

In-Class Protocol / Reminders

Ensure strict adherence to these pedagogical steps for every session.

  • Maintain Professionalism at All Times: Stay focused, follow classroom standards, and present yourself professionally in every class.
  • Zero Tolerance for Bullying, Harassment, or Cheating: Any form of bullying, disrespect, or dishonest behavior toward students or colleagues is strictly prohibited.
  • Ensure Accurate Assessments: Always double-check your assessment before submitting to ensure accuracy and correctness.
  • Verify Lesson Materials Before Starting the Class: Always confirm the correct curriculum, unit number, lesson number, and slide number to avoid teaching the wrong material.
  • Follow Correct Class Tagging Guidelines:
    • ✔ TAG AS COMPLETE when: 25-min class lasts 20 minutes or more | 50-min class lasts 40 minutes or more
    • ❌ TAG AS INCOMPLETE when: - Student absence with notice - Absence without notice - Class duration is below the required minutes (20/40), regardless of reason
  • Check Audio, Camera, and Classroom Energy: Test your devices before class, keep your camera properly positioned, and start every lesson with a smile to create a welcoming learning environment.
  • Privacy and Conflict of Interest Policy: To maintain professional standards, teachers must refrain from disclosing personal contact details or social media profiles to students. Additionally, providing private tutoring or lessons to current students is strictly prohibited. Non-compliance with these boundary guidelines will result in formal disciplinary action.
